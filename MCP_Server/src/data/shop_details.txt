About Us:
Brew Buddy Coffee Shop is a cozy, community-focused café specialising in
hand-crafted espresso beverages, single-origin pour-overs, and freshly baked
pastries. We source our beans from sustainable farms across Ethiopia, Colombia,
and Guatemala, roasted locally every week to ensure peak freshness.
